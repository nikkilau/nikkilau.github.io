Current research

Kernelized Bandits with Offline Information

Working paper. Project lead, November 2025–present.

Historical observations can be useful even when their labels are biased or they cover only part of the decision space. I developed Safe Kernelized MIN-UCB, which combines three upper confidence bounds, including one based entirely on online data. I derived regret bounds and tested how bias and coverage affect the value of offline data.

RMEvolve: Algorithm Evolution for Network Revenue Management

Collaborative research project. Team member, May 2026–present.

We use large language models to generate rules for accepting orders under capacity constraints. On some instances, the best evolved policies improved revenue by about 2% over the strongest baseline. Gains varied on held-out sales periods. We are studying which rules generalize as demand changes.
