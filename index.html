Selected research

Online learning · Working paper

Kernelized Bandits with Offline Information

When can historical data reduce the cost of exploration? I developed Safe Kernelized MIN-UCB to use offline observations while accounting for biased labels and gaps in coverage.

Research details

Project lead · November 2025–present

The algorithm takes the smallest of three upper confidence bounds, one based only on online data. I derived regret bounds and tested how bias and coverage affect the value of historical data in finite-arm, linear-kernel and RBF-kernel settings.

Revenue management · Collaborative project

RMEvolve: Algorithm Evolution for Network Revenue Management

Can a language model discover better rules for accepting orders when capacity is limited? Our team evolves candidate policies and tests them on sales periods held out during training.

Research details

Team member · May 2026–present

On some instances, evolved policies improved revenue by up to about 2% over the best baseline. Gains varied on held-out periods. We study which policies hold up under seasonal demand, trends and sudden shifts.

Earlier projects

2024Physics-informed neural networks for option pricing

I led a course project on the Black–Scholes equation. I incorporated the equation and its initial and boundary conditions into a neural network’s training objective, then compared European call and put prices with analytical solutions.

2023–24Ant colony optimization for binary problems

I implemented ant colony algorithms in MATLAB for 0–1 knapsack and unconstrained binary quadratic problems. Across 50 benchmark instances, the average error stayed within 1%.

2023Understanding parking experiences through sentiment analysis

I led a student research team that identified roughly 50,000 parking-related records and combined sentiment analysis with a survey of drivers’ experiences. The project received municipal-level recognition in Shanghai.
